# The Council

- **Date:** 2026-11-09. **Pages:** [open questions](https://claude.ai/artifact/JRRFM9rYWv1Gbkc7cKrRym), [concepts](https://claude.ai/artifact/E57hx6ZSBmzf15F45XUtot), [round 1](https://claude.ai/artifact/Drq7sEeVNgVW3YJD4Uy3xQ). **Agent cost:** $0.64 measured (research $0.36, critique $0.28)

## Pitch
A fantasy council in a Claude chat. Morgan leads it, and 12 councillors sit with them. Each councillor is the physical form of one aspect of Morgan, and some exist to challenge the others. Morgan brings a question, a few councillors argue it in short speeches, a challenger opposes, and Morgan rules. The point is to reach the "oh right" moment faster, and to learn what Morgan's fundamental aspects are and how they reflect each other.

## Who it's for
Morgan, in Claude chats (often on a phone). Others may see it. The format should be copyable later, but it is built for Morgan (round 1).

## Core loop or idea
1. Morgan opens a sitting and states a topic or problem "to the best of my ability".
2. A Herald calls the 3 most relevant councillors. The challenger is the seat opposite the first speaker.
3. Each speaks in a few lines. The challenger cross-examines. A councillor may not concede without a new reason.
4. A Scribe states the one insight and a numbered decree line Morgan can keep. Morgan rules, and can summon anyone or call another round.

The seats are found in Morgan through scenario questions (B). Each seat cites the answers it came from. The seats are checked against Schwartz's values circle for gaps and opposites (A). Sittings are short by law (C).

## What it isn't
- "Boring to read or useless or too long winded" (Morgan).
- Not therapy. It is a thinking tool, with a crisis rule that stops a sitting and points to real help.
- Not a textbook personality model: the values circle is a checklist, not the seat map.
- Not an app, for now. It starts in Claude chats.

## Kill criteria
- In the first test, 5 real stuck questions run as a plain "3 views, 2 lines each" prompt and as a Council sitting: if Morgan can't tell which is better, stop building.
- In 4 weeks after launch, fewer than 4 sittings.
- Morgan skims or skips speeches, which means they're too long.
- In most sittings, the councillors end up agreeing with Morgan's opening view.
- After 3 sittings, Morgan can't tell the councillors apart by voice.

## Design brief
**Settled:**
- 12 councillors plus Morgan, who leads them (13, Morgan's favourite number).
- Each councillor's looks reflect their aspect (a fantasy race or human form).
- The fantasy is whimsy, not the point.
- It runs in Claude chats.
- The loop is the one above, short by law, with the opposite-seat challenger and a decree line.
- Seats come from Morgan, never added or changed by Claude without Morgan's yes.
- Challengers argue from real values (Nemeth: authentic dissent beats assigned dissent).
- Councillors address Morgan by name or title (self-distancing, Kross).

**For design to work out:**
1. Draw out Morgan's aspects with scenario questions. Morgan said "I'm honestly bad at this and need help": don't ask for a list cold. Starting hints from Morgan: wisdom, intelligence, kindness, emotions, self-interest.
2. Build the 12 from the answers, and check them against the values circle.
3. Pair the opposites.
4. Give each one a name, race, look, symbol and speaking voice.
5. Write the procedure and length limits.
6. Write the crisis rule.
7. Decide the home (a Claude Project or a skill) and the charter format.

**Do not reopen:** the seat count, Claude chats as the home, short sittings, or Claude inventing seats.
