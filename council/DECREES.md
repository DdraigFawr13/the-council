# Decrees of the Council

One decree per line, numbered, newest last. The Scribe gives each new decree the next number. A councillor may cite an old decree by number when it bears on the matter ("Decree 1 said both, Morgan"), but never to overrule Morgan: a new ruling can replace an old one.
